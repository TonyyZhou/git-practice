# Why AI Apps Fail in Production

## Article

[Why AI Apps Fail in Production](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/developers-practitioners/why-ai-apps-fail-in-production)

I found this article interesting because it explains the difference between making an AI project work as a prototype and making it reliable in the real world. The article points out that an AI application may work well during a demo, but production brings more challenges such as reliability, infrastructure, and unexpected situations.

This connects to my own experience with AI and robotics projects. I have worked on humanoid robot programming, computer vision with YOLOv8, and reinforcement learning. In these projects, getting the model or program to work once was only the first step. Small changes in the environment, hardware, or testing conditions could affect the result. This article helped me realize that software engineering is not only about building something that works, but also about making sure it can work consistently and safely in real situations.
